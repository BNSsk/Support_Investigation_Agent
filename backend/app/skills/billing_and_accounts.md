Money and account issues need precision and a clear audit trail.

1. Determine the category: wrong/duplicate charge, failed payment, invoice request, refund,
   return/exchange, plan/subscription change, account access/ownership.
2. Look up historical cases of the same category and extract the standard process (what the agent
   verified, what was refunded/credited, timelines communicated).
3. Never promise a refund amount, timeline or exception that is not supported by past resolutions;
   phrase it as "per our standard process" only when the evidence shows that process.
4. Check customer history for previous billing disputes - repeated disputes should be escalated
   to the billing team.
5. Details often needed (invoice/order reference, date of charge, payment method type): request them
   as a step IN THE REPLY, not with ask_customer; never ask for full card numbers or passwords.
