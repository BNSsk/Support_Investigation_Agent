---
name: escalation
description: Decide when a case must be escalated and write a precise hand-off for the receiving team.
stage: escalate
---
Escalate when ANY of these hold:
- Security breach, data loss/exposure, legal or HR-sensitive matter.
- Outage or degradation affecting multiple users / business-critical operations with no proven fix.
- High priority and the historical evidence does not contain a reliable resolution.
- Evidence conflicts and the customer could not (or did not) resolve the ambiguity.
- Recurring issue: the customer has previous cases about the same problem.
- The resolution needs an approval, refund exception, or access the support agent does not have.
<!-- brief-end -->

Hand-off format (internal):
1. One-line problem statement (who / what / impact).
2. What is known: classification, key facts from the ticket and clarifications.
3. Evidence reviewed: case IDs with one-line takeaways, and why they were not sufficient.
4. Suggested next step for the receiving team.
Target team = the most appropriate queue from the dataset labels.

Customer holding reply: acknowledge, state that a specialist team is taking over, set expectations
without promising timelines that are not in evidence.
